# Day One Biopharmaceuticals

> Commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company developing and commercializing targeted therapies for people of all ages with life-threatening diseases, with an initial focus on pediatric and rare cancers. Lead product OJEMDA (tovorafenib) is FDA-approved for relapsed or refractory pediatric low-grade glioma; pipeline includes DAY301 and the antibody-drug conjugate Emi-Le. Headquartered in Brisbane, California, and now part of the Servier Group (acquired March 2026). No public developer API or SDK is published.
